### 1. **Core Formulation: TLF as a Multi-Objective Penalty**

$$L = \sum_{i} w_i \cdot l_i(x)$$

$$TLF = L + \lambda \cdot R_{tragic}$$

$R_{tragic}$ is the sum of penalties for ignoring constraints:

$$R_{tragic} = \alpha \cdot Irr(x) + \beta \cdot Cost(x) + \gamma \cdot Obs(x) + \delta \cdot Fin(x)$$
